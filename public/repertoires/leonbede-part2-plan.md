# Part 2 — plans and decisions

Nine courses with 36 sourced continuations and 18 decision drills. Chapters 01–04 follow full lines; 05–06 start from FEN positions. The app uses full depth for these courses.

## Part 2 · Slav and Semi-Slav

Classical Slav: central control · Exchange Slav: development and material safety · Meran: calculating central play · Semi-Slav: development before the central break

- Development and the d-file support a central break. After ...c5, count attackers and defenders before taking a pawn.
- Balance pressure on b7 with the defence of c3 and king safety. Check the opposing bishop’s diagonal before placing a rook on it.
- Central breaks and sacrifices in the Meran require calculation. Checks and material recovery take priority over general rules.

## Part 2 · Other defences to 1.d4

Englund: development with tactical awareness · Nimzo-Indian: the classical Qc2 system · King’s Indian: the Bayonet system · Grünfeld: supporting an active centre

- Develop with tempo against an early queen and check every capture. Do not castle mechanically when a tactic is available.
- Qc2 belongs to the classical system. Respond to ...d5 according to central tension; the bishop pair is not the only goal.
- In the Bayonet, answer ...a5 and reassess central tension after ...f5. Support pawn advances with pieces.
